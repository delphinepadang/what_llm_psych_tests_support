Supplementary Fig. S14. Language-effect direction across repeated runs (formerly Figure 2C). Each tile identifies one model and its sign pattern for Chinese-minus-English mean scores across the five runs. Blue: Chinese scores higher in every run; light blue: Chinese scores at least as high in every run with a tie; vermillion: both positive and negative differences occur; grey: English scores at least as high in every run. Category counts are calculated from the raw responses, not manually assigned. Means use available valid rescored items in each language-run combination.
