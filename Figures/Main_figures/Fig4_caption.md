Fig. 4. Relationship between accuracy and response variability. Each point represents one of 21 models on the Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test. Accuracy is the mean proportion of correct responses, first averaged within each model-item-condition combination across valid responses and then across the 72 item-condition combinations (36 items with and without word definitions). Response variability is the percentage of those combinations with more than one distinct answer across repeated runs. Each item was administered in five runs; eight combinations had four valid responses, while the remaining 1,504 had five. Spearman's rank correlation was 0.05. The dashed vertical line marks the adult reference mean of 26.2/36 (72.8%), retained from the original figure (Baron-Cohen et al., 2001).
