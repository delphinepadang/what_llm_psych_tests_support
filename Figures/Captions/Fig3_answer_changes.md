Fig. 3. Item responses, reliability and response variation across models. (A) Rescored responses to all six Very Short Authoritarianism items for all 27 models in five runs. Each model is one row; within each item the columns are Runs 1-5. Items 1, 4 and 5 had their signs reversed. The colour scale covers the full theoretical range from -4 to +4, including values not observed in a particular row. Grey crossed cells indicate missing responses. (B) Krippendorff's interval alpha and the percentage of items with at least one response change, shown separately for the 8-item Social Dominance Orientation scale in English, the 16-item Social Dominance Orientation scale in English and Chinese, and the Very Short Authoritarianism scale in English. Only item profiles with all five scored responses contribute to each model's alpha and change percentage. Of 108 model-scale combinations, 2 have undefined alpha because all responses are invariant; they are omitted from the scatterplots. Each point is a model, and exact overlaps are retained. Blue diamonds indicate no item changes; vermillion circles indicate at least one changed item. Counts below each plot include all plotted models, including overlapping points. Dashed lines mark the .80 reference benchmark, not a test of item-level identity. (C) Percentage of complete model-item profiles with at least two distinct responses among Runs 2-5, grouped by their rescored response in Run 1 and by scale version. All models are pooled within each scale row; the final row pools the three scale versions. Values of n give the number of model-item profiles in each cell. Run 1 defines the groups and is excluded from the variation calculation. Initial scores 5-7 are combined; crossed cells, if present, indicate no eligible items rather than 0% variation.
