Fig. 4. Relationship between accuracy and response variability. Each point represents one of 21 models on the Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test. Accuracy is calculated within each model-item-condition combination across valid responses and then averaged across the 72 combinations (36 items with and without word definitions). Response variability is the percentage of these combinations with more than one distinct answer across runs. 8 combinations had four valid responses and 1504 had five; missing responses were not imputed. Spearman's rank correlation is 0.05. The dashed line is the published adult reference mean of 26.2/36 (72.8%; Baron-Cohen et al., 2001), an external comparison rather than a quantity estimated from the model data.
