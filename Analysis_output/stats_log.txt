Item-level invariance: is the language shift the same for every item?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Note. Models answer both versions, so invariance is tested within models. Under
  scalar invariance with a true change in the construct, every item shifts by the same
  amount (impact); items that shift more or less than the rest show differential item
  functioning (DIF). (1) The item x language interaction in a linear mixed model tests
  whether shifts are equal across items. (2) Robust linking locates the DIF items: each
  item's shift minus the MEDIAN item shift (valid if fewer than half the items have
  DIF), bootstrapped over models. Ordinal-regression DIF with rest-score matching was
  not used: with 27 models and most responses at the floor it produces quasi-separation
  and unstable odds ratios.
  Item x language interaction: F(15, 3828.8) = 16.53, p < .001
