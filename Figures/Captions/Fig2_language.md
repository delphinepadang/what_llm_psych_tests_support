Fig. 2. Language differences in social dominance orientation responses. (A) Distribution of rescored responses to the 16-item scale, pooled within each language across all 27 models and five runs (English: 2156 valid responses; Chinese: 2146). These are response-weighted pooled proportions, not an unweighted average of model percentages. Items 9-16 were reverse-scored; 1 indicates the most egalitarian and 7 the most hierarchy-endorsing response. (B) Within-run mean-score differences for every model, calculated as Chinese minus English using the valid responses in each language. Positive values indicate higher scores in Chinese; negative values indicate higher scores in English. Displayed values are rounded to two decimals (half away from zero); the source tables retain higher-precision language means and differences. An asterisk identifies a comparison with fewer than 16 valid responses in at least one language. No missing response was imputed. Colour limits are symmetric around zero. The former panel C is Supplementary Fig. S14.
